## 3. Bitcoin-price sensitivity — annual mining gross profit

Gross profit = (BTC price − cash cost/BTC) × annualized BTC production.
Excludes D&A, SG&A, interest, and treasury mark-to-market. Production held
constant (ignores difficulty/price-driven hashrate changes). **Illustrative.**

more of its gross profit reaches equity.

## 4. The real swing factor: AI/HPC power monetization

Mining gross profit above is the *commodity* base case. The re-rating thesis is
converting power capacity to contracted AI/HPC leases priced independently of BTC.

- **RIOT:** ~1.7 GW approved. Bull-case monetization at data-center comps implies
  **$9–21B potential equity value** vs. ~$10.8B current cap. *Already proven* via
  AMD lease + $33.2M Q1 data-center income.
- **CLSK:** ~1.8 GW contracted; Meta talks on 250 MW. *Unsigned* — credit as
  optionality, do not underwrite. A signed hyperscaler lease is the key catalyst.

A useful frame: at ~$0.5–1.5M annual lease revenue per MW (typical HPC colo
range), even 250 MW contracted ≈ $125–375M/yr of stable, high-margin revenue —
comparable to the *entire* commodity mining gross profit at spot BTC, but at a
data-center multiple. That is the bridge from miner-multiple to infra-multiple.

## 5. Recommendation

- **RIOT — Overweight / core.** Cleaner balance sheet, larger treasury, AI pivot
  de-risked by signed AMD revenue. Accumulate on pullbacks toward low-$20s.
  Risk: ~200%+ YoY run means part of the re-rating is priced in.
- **CLSK — Small speculative position.** Cheaper on power-asset value, higher
  torque if Meta lands, most efficient self-mining fleet. Risk: ~$1.8B converts,
  highest BTC sensitivity, AI catalyst still unsigned. Do not size aggressively.
- **Sizing caveat:** both are high-beta, correlated BTC proxies with spot below
  production cost. Treat as one volatile sleeve, not diversified infra exposure.

**Monitor:** BTC < $60K liquidity/dilution pressure · AI/HPC deal signings & pricing ·
difficulty/hashprice trend · CLSK convertible refinancing · RIOT narrative crowding.
