# Manuscript outline (working)

**Working title:** Occult contralateral nodal metastasis and contralateral neck failure after transoral surgery for
HPV-associated oropharyngeal carcinoma: implications for unilateral treatment of multinodal disease

1. **Introduction:** The ipsilateral-RT paradigm (PMH and others) and its extension to N2b. Surveys showing practice
   still defaults to bilateral treatment. HN.11 and lymphoscintigraphy. The gap: almost no pathologic ground truth
   for the contralateral neck in HPV+ disease, stratified by ipsilateral nodal burden.
2. **Methods:** Cohorts A/B, the lateralized definition, blinded re-read, RT plan-derived contralateral dose,
   endpoints, SAP.
3. **Results:**
   - Fig 1: CONSORT-style flow
   - Table 1: cohort characteristics
   - Table 2: occult rate by indication and by ipsilateral burden
   - Fig 2: cumulative incidence of CRF by contralateral RT
   - Table 3: inter-rater κ
4. **Discussion:** What the data justify (an estimate with its CI) and what they don't (a safety claim at this n).
   Fit with HN.11. Need for pooling.
5. **Target journals:** JAMA Otolaryngol HNS; Oral Oncol; IJROBP; Head Neck
