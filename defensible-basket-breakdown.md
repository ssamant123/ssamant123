## Why CLSK/RIOT alone is the *less* defensible bet
- Mostly **unproven optionality** (CLSK has $0 signed AI revenue).
- **Naked Bitcoin beta** — little contracted, BTC-decoupled cashflow to cushion drawdowns.
- **Concentration** in two correlated names with one shared driver.

## The honest trade-off (defensibility isn't free)
- **You pay up for the floor.** IREN/APLD at $9–16B/GW vs. CLSK's $2.5B/GW. The basket
  **lowers variance but caps torque** — if the conversion catalyst hits, concentrated
  CLSK/RIOT has more upside. Defensible = higher floor, lower ceiling.
- **Shallow diversification.** All five remain levered to (a) the AI-capex cycle and
  (b) Bitcoin (mining bases). CORZ/APLD add **CoreWeave-solvency** dependence. True
  diversification means going *outside* this theme.
- **CORZ is the linchpin** of the upgrade — underwrite its single-tenant (CoreWeave)
  concentration; it's contracted revenue (beats CLSK's zero) but one leveraged customer.

**Bottom line:** A CORZ/IREN-anchored 5-name basket is more *defensible* than CLSK/RIOT
alone — swapping torque for contracted, counterparty-diversified cashflow. Choose it for
risk-adjusted defensibility; keep the concentrated 2-name play only if the goal is
maximum torque to the AI-conversion catalyst.
