## Read for the sleeve

- **IREN — quality leader.** Furthest along, best counterparties (Microsoft + NVIDIA,
  not a single leveraged tenant), fastest execution, ~85% project EBITDA on the
  Microsoft deal. **Different business though** — owns GPUs and sells *cloud*, not just
  leasing power: more upside, more capex/tech risk, trades ~2× valuation/MW of BTC-heavy
  peers. Natural "quality" diversifier to the two-name CLSK/RIOT concentration.
- **Core Scientific — verify before touching.** Reporting indicates CoreWeave acquired
  it for ~$9B in stock. If so, CORZ is no longer an independent equity — effectively a
  merger-arb / CoreWeave proxy, not a clean power-landlord long. **Confirm on EDGAR
  first.**
- **Applied Digital — highest torque, highest single-point risk.** Purest landlord
  story (~$11B contracted CoreWeave leases, 1+ GW pipeline) at the smallest cap
  (~$2.3B). But **nearly all AI revenue rides on CoreWeave** — itself a leveraged
  AI-cloud company. If CoreWeave wobbles, APLD wobbles.

## Cross-cutting insight
CORZ and APLD are bets **on CoreWeave** as much as on AI infrastructure. IREN spreads
counterparty risk across Microsoft + NVIDIA. CLSK/RIOT's AMD/Meta exposure is smaller
but **not** CoreWeave-dependent. Counterparty quality is the main axis separating these.

## If diversifying the sleeve
For a speculative sleeve with high risk tolerance, **IREN** is the cleanest "quality"
add (proven, diversified counterparties) and **APLD** the cleanest "torque" add (small
cap, pure landlord) — at the cost of CoreWeave concentration. **CORZ** is on hold
pending acquisition-status confirmation. None of this changes the CLSK/RIOT plan; it
widens the menu.
