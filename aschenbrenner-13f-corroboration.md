## Read-through for the CLSK/RIOT deployment
**Confirmation:** a high-profile, well-capitalized AI-infra fund independently arrived at
the "power is the bottleneck" thesis and is long the exact miners we're deploying into.

**But weigh it honestly — this is confirmation, not validation:**
1. **Lagged & fluid.** 13F is filed up to 45 days after quarter-end; the book *tripled*
   in one quarter, so current positioning may differ (he may have trimmed).
2. **His structure is hedged; ours isn't.** The long-miner bet is *paired with* an $8.5B
   chipmaker-put hedge — a long-infra / short-silicon pair. A naked long of CLSK/RIOT
   carries the AI-drawdown risk his puts are designed to offset.
3. **Don't anchor on a famous name.** Crowded "smart-money" longs can unwind hard;
   treat this as one input, not a thesis crutch.
